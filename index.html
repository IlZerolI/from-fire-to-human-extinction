From Fire to Human Extinction

Every innovation that lifted civilisation also carries the seed of how we might end. Nine breakthroughs. Nine groups. One question: can we survive our own ingenuity?

Premise

Progress has a price

Fire fed us. Industry powered us. The web connected us. AI thinks for us. Each leap forward created a new way to fall.

Promise

What we gained

Each breakthrough expanded human potential—longer lives, more knowledge, greater power over nature.

Peril

What we risk

Nuclear war, climate collapse, engineered pathogens, misaligned intelligence—the same tools turned inward.

Structure

Nine groups of three

Each group owns one innovation: its rise, its promise, and its path to extinction.

Question

Can we control it?

The next century decides whether we master our inventions or become extinct by them.

"We are the first species to hold our own extinction in our hands—and the first to have the power to prevent it." Presentation Theme
